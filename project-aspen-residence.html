About the project

A modern mountain home where light and luxury meet

This Aspen residence called for a refined modern aesthetic that could hold its own against the drama of the surrounding landscape — Calacatta marble fireplace walls soaring to the ceiling, velvet and chrome furnishings that feel both collected and considered.

Every material decision was made with the Roaring Fork Valley in mind. The palette draws from the mountain environment — cool silvers, slate blues, and warm creams that shift with the quality of alpine light throughout the day.

The lower-level entertainment lounge presented its own story entirely: a richer, moodier register with aged brass, antiqued mirrors, and a dramatic tiered chandelier that anchors the space in a different kind of luxury — the kind that comes out after dark.
